# **1\. Executive Summary**

This playbook supersedes the three prior Esteemed Digital local web services documents (the original Olympia plan, the Olympia \+ Oceanic reframe, and the multi-channel acquisition addendum). It consolidates the strategic decisions made since those drafts, incorporates three live institutional anchors, formalizes a Drupal migration service line, and forks the channel mix by customer tier.

The thesis is simple: Esteemed Digital is the customer-facing brand for local and vertical web services across three customer tiers, all delivered on the Oceanic platform. The brand carries 15+ years of accumulated trust from the Drupal community, Celebrate Drupal heritage, the WA Veterans Affairs work, the Esteemed Colleagues talent network, and the Drupal Jobs community service. Oceanic stays in the background as infrastructure — visible only through a "Built on Oceanic by Cetacean Labs" co-brand — preserving Cetacean's enterprise pricing power while still rolling up local customer count as platform validation for the Series A.

Three customer tiers are now in play simultaneously. Tier 1 is Drupal migration, productized for SMB and Mid-market with public pricing and a self-serve funnel, plus a consultative enterprise track Tom Schmidt leads. Tier 2 is new SMB and nonprofit builds — the Lovable-for-business motion using the Oceanic App Builder, beachheaded in Olympia and expanding regionally. Tier 3 is mid-large institutional, anchored by three live customers — IEEE, Alvernia University, and the Cambridge Redevelopment Authority (State of MA) — and grown through Tom's executive network.

The five-channel prospecting motion (cold email, LinkedIn, direct mail, door-to-door, and Google Ads) runs through the Oceanic CRM, with channel mix forked by tier. SMS is excluded from prospecting on TCPA grounds and reserved for post-sale notifications. Direct mail shares operational rails with the Great Lawns brand.

# **2\. Brand Decision and Positioning**

## **2.1 Esteemed Digital, customer-facing**

The brand decision is to keep Esteemed Digital as the customer-facing identity, not to rename the unit to Oceanic. Three reasons drive this:

* **Pricing-anchor protection.** Oceanic's enterprise products are positioned at $1.5K to $10K per month for Echo and $150K setup plus $14,998 per month for Orca Enterprise. A public $149 per month SMB website tier under the Oceanic name would create a permanent procurement-search anchor that compresses enterprise deal value. Glean and Palantir don't operate this way for a reason.

* **ICP separation.** The Coherence Advisory campaign explicitly excludes web design and marketing agencies from its Apollo filter. Letting Oceanic also run local web design muddies that signal for the technical buyers — Heads of AI, VP Engineering, CTOs — the AI Advisory campaign is built to attract.

* **Heritage equity is real.** Celebrate Drupal (2011–2019), the WA Veterans Affairs engagement, the Olympia Drupal meetup, the Esteemed Colleagues network of 35,000+ vetted IT professionals, and the Drupal Jobs initiative are all under the Esteemed umbrella. Discarding the brand discards the narrative.

This mirrors the Acquia / Drupal pattern: Acquia hosts Drupal sites for Fortune 500 and government, and is open about the relationship, but no one confuses Drupal core with Acquia hosting. The infrastructure brand stays distinct from the customer-facing brand. It is also the same multi-brand pattern Cetacean already operates with Great Lawns.

## **2.2 Co-brand: "Built on Oceanic by Cetacean Labs"**

Oceanic is acknowledged as the platform on every page of the Esteemed Digital site, in all customer-facing materials, and at strength on the AI add-on pages where the platform value is real. The treatment escalates with relevance:

* Site footer and About page: "Built on Oceanic by Cetacean Labs" with link to oceanicai.io.

* Product pages: small Oceanic mark, brief platform description.

* AI add-on pages (Voice CRM, Social, Blog-in-Your-Voice, Agents): "Powered by Oceanic Intelligence" prominent — this is where the platform horsepower is the differentiator.

* Series A and investor materials: full Oceanic platform validation narrative built on Esteemed Digital customer count.

## **2.3 Positioning statement**

*"Esteemed Digital is Lovable for businesses that need someone to host it, support it, and grow it with them. We build, we host, we maintain, and we grow your site with you using AI that actually sounds like you."*

The Lovable comparison is intentional — it borrows the magic of describe-it-and-get-it from a brand small business owners are starting to recognize, and adds the trust signals (hosting, support, longevity) Lovable does not provide. The implicit contrast is also against WordPress agencies (build-and-leave) and against Wix or Squarespace (build-it-yourself with no support).

## **2.4 Domain and digital presence**

Primary domain: digital.esteemed.io (current). All landing pages, the migration funnel, the hosting plans page, and the AI add-on catalog live under this subdomain. The parent esteemed.io continues to host the Esteemed Colleagues talent platform, the Drupal Jobs initiative, and the broader corporate identity. Cross-linking is natural and reinforces the heritage.

# **5\. Tier 1 — Drupal Migration Service Line**

## **5.1 Why this is the strongest wedge in the plan**

Drupal 7 reached end-of-life in January 2025\. Drupal 9 reached end-of-life in November 2023\. There is a long tail of organizations — small businesses, nonprofits, higher education, state and local government — running unsupported Drupal that knows it needs to move and is dreading the project. The migration math is genuinely difficult, the Drupal-to-Drupal upgrade story is unappealing for non-technical organizations, and the platform alternatives (WordPress, Wix, Squarespace) feel like downgrades to anyone who chose Drupal originally.

Esteemed Digital is uniquely positioned to be the migration partner for these organizations. The pitch is simple and authentic: "We helped build Drupal. We'll help you move beyond it — at your pace, on your terms, onto a modern React \+ PostgreSQL stack we host and support."

## **5.2 Hybrid model: productized SMB/Mid \+ consultative Enterprise**

SMB and Mid-market migrations are productized with fixed scope, public pricing, and a self-serve evaluation funnel at digital.esteemed.io/drupal-migration. Enterprise migrations are consultative, scoped per engagement, and Tom-led.

# **7\. Tier 3 — Mid-Large Institutional**

IEEE, Alvernia University, and the Cambridge Redevelopment Authority (State of Massachusetts) are the validation base. RFP responses to state, local, and federal agencies are upside, not strategy. The plan does not depend on RFP wins for revenue projections or runway. When a contract lands, it is incremental. This framing protects the team from the morale swings inherent in RFP-driven pipelines.

The exception is RFPs where Chris's existing relationships (WA state from the Veterans Affairs era) or Tom's network create genuine inside positioning. Those are worth the response cost. Cold RFP responses are not.

# **8\. Channel Strategy**

## **8.1 Forked by tier, unified by Oceanic CRM**

The five-channel prospecting motion (cold email \+ LinkedIn \+ direct mail \+ door-to-door \+ Google Ads) runs through the Oceanic CRM. Channel selection forks by customer tier. | **SMS** | Post-sale only (TCPA risk) | Post-sale only (TCPA risk) | Post-sale only |

## **8.2 Why SMS is excluded from prospecting**

Cold SMS to small businesses is a TCPA liability. Penalties run $500–$1,500 per unconsented text and small businesses litigate. SMS is reserved for post-sale notifications: appointment confirmations from the Voice CRM agent, support ticket updates, AI feature alerts. This is well within the existing customer relationship and low-risk.

## **8.3 Direct mail rails shared with Great Lawns**

Esteemed Digital and Great Lawns are both adopting direct mail in 2026\. The print vendor, the address-scrub workflow, the personalized-audit template logic, and the mailing cadence are shared operational infrastructure. Creative differs by brand; rails are shared. This roughly halves the per-piece operational overhead for both brands.

## **8.4 Door-to-door — the differentiator that holds**

Two walks per week, 15-20 businesses per session, with a personalized printed audit in hand. Nobody else in the Olympia market does this. It is disarming, memorable, and generates warm callback rates of 8-15%. The Oceanic App Builder makes the audit-to-prototype turnaround fast enough to follow a walk on Monday with a working demo by Friday — a level of speed no WordPress freelancer can match.

# **10\. Series A Narrative**

The local SMB count, the migration win count, and the institutional anchor list all roll up as a single Series A story: Oceanic platform validation across three customer scales, with Esteemed Digital as the customer-facing demonstration that the platform works for real businesses across the size spectrum.

The honest investor narrative looks roughly like this:

1. Oceanic is the multi-cloud AI platform — Trident MCP live, Echo RAG launching, App Builder in production through Esteemed Digital.

2. Esteemed Digital is one of the demonstration customers and a customer acquisition channel into Oceanic. N customers (target: 80-100 by month 12\) on the platform, plus Drupal migrations bringing institutional buyers onto the same stack.

3. Three live institutional anchors (IEEE, Alvernia, Cambridge Redevelopment Authority) prove the higher-tier motion before the Series A close.

4. The AI Advisory campaign is the parallel enterprise motion targeting net-new technical buyers; Esteemed Digital is the local and vertical motion that does not compete with it for ICP.

5. Combined: a credible multi-segment platform validation story, not a single-bet enterprise pitch.

The platform-validation count is the multiplier. Investors care less about $349K of Tier 1+2 revenue than they do about "100+ businesses are paying for Oceanic infrastructure" because the latter is what makes the enterprise pitch credible.

By the time the Olympia and Tacoma models are proven and documented as a repeatable playbook, the franchise option becomes credible. Esteemed Digital becomes a channel program: regional partners license the playbook, the Oceanic platform, and the brand, and operate locally under the Esteemed Digital banner.
