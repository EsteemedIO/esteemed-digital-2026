## SECTION 8 — FAQ

**Section header:** Honest answers to the questions you're going to ask

**Accordion-style. Eight questions:**

**Q: Is the migration really free?** For most small business websites moving onto a 12-month hosting agreement, yes. Larger sites (more than \~150 pages, complex e-commerce, multiple integrations) get a fixed-price quote after the free assessment. We'll never surprise you with a bill.

**Q: What if I want to leave later?** You can. We give you an export of your content and help you move to wherever you're going. We don't believe in lock-in through hostage data.

**Q: Will my SEO survive the migration?** Yes. We map every old URL to a new one with proper 301 redirects, preserve your meta tags, your structured data, your alt text, all of it. Most clients see SEO improve after migration because the new site loads faster and is built for modern search.

**Q: Can I still have a designer make custom changes?** Yes. The prompt-driven editor handles content and layout changes. Your designer (yours, ours, or anyone's) handles brand work, custom illustration, anything that needs a human eye. We're not trying to replace designers. We're trying to replace the part where you wait three weeks to update your phone number.

**Q: What happens to my domain and email?** Your domain stays yours, registered where it is now (we don't take ownership). Your email stays where it is — Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, wherever. We only host your website.

**Q: Drupal 7 / Drupal 9 / WordPress — which do you handle?** All of them. WordPress (any version), Drupal 7 through 11, plus older custom CMSes. The migration process is the same: we extract your content, rebuild on Oceanic, you approve.

**Q: How long does a migration take?** Most small business sites: 1-3 weeks from "yes" to launch. Larger sites: 4-8 weeks. We'll give you a real timeline after the free assessment, and we hit it.

**Q: What's the catch?** The catch is the 12-month hosting agreement. We make our money on hosting and support, not on migration project fees. If you don't want to host with us long-term, the migration won't be free — but the math still usually works out cheaper than your current setup.

## TECHNICAL / DESIGN NOTES FOR THE WEB TEAM

**Above the fold must contain:**

1. Headline  
2. Subhead  
3. Primary CTA button  
4. Trust line (IEEE / Alvernia / Cambridge)

Nothing else. The visitor decides whether to scroll based on those four elements.

**Demo video specs:**

- Format: MP4, H.264  
- Length: 30-60 seconds (closer to 30 is better)  
- Autoplay: muted (browser requirement)  
- Captions: yes, baked in (most viewers won't unmute)  
- Hosting: serve from Oceanic CDN, not YouTube — no recommended-videos rabbit hole at end  
- Fallback: if video fails to load, show static "see the demo" CTA that opens in modal

**Form integration:** Form submits into Oceanic CRM. Tag automatically with campaign source (cold email, LinkedIn, Google Ads, direct, referral). Trigger a Slack notification to Chris within 60 seconds of submission so the call can be booked same-day if possible.

**Mobile considerations:**

- Hero stacks vertically; demo plays full-width  
- Pricing cards stack to single column  
- FAQ accordion preserves on mobile  
- CTA button sticky on scroll (always one tap away)

**Performance budget:**

- Target Largest Contentful Paint \< 2.0s  
- Demo video lazy-loaded (only loads when user scrolls to it)  
- No external trackers above the fold

**SEO targets:**

- Primary keywords: "WordPress migration", "Drupal migration", "AI website builder", "talk to your website"  
- Meta description: "Migrate your WordPress or Drupal site to Esteemed Digital. Edit your site by talking to it. Hosted, supported, and grown with you. Trusted by IEEE."

**A/B test plan (post-launch):**

- Test 1: Hero headline (primary vs. Option C)  
- Test 2: Demo placement (above pricing vs. below pricing)  
- Test 3: Form length (3 fields vs. 5 fields with name \+ phone)
