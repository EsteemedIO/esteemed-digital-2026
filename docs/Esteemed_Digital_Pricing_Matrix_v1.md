# **1\. Pricing Philosophy**

Esteemed Digital prices to be "the same or slightly less than your current agency, with a dramatically better product." The pricing is value-led, not price-led. Every published number in this document maps to a competitor benchmark — we are positioned to be in the same range as a typical WordPress agency or marketing services firm, with the differentiator being the product (prompt-driven editing, Trident-powered AI, single integrated stack) rather than the price.

There are three reasons we don't compete on price:

* **Anchor risk.** If we are known as the cheap option, we permanently cap our pricing power. The AI ladder ($99-$329/mo) gets squeezed because customers think of us as discount.

* **Product differentiation.** The prompt-driven Lovable experience is genuinely a different product. Pricing it as cheaper undersells what we built.

* **Margin protection.** The unit economics need real margin to fund the AI features that drive long-term retention. Cutting hosting price by 70% to win the deal eliminates the budget for the AI investment.

Customers paying less than $200/mo at their current agency self-select into Starter Hosting at $149/mo and save money. Customers paying $400-800/mo land at Growth or Pro and pay roughly the same for a substantially better product. The savings story shows up organically in customer testimonials, not in our headline copy.

The pricing structure is designed around a deliberate ladder: customers enter cheap, experience the prompt-driven product, add Trident AI services as they see ROI, and graduate into full digital marketing consolidation over 12-18 months.

| Stage | Customer State | Typical Spend | Our Position |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Migration** | Frustrated with current WordPress agency or dead Drupal site. Sees the demo, books call. | $0 migration \+ $149-$249 hosting starting month 1 | Free migration is the entry. Hosting agreement is the commitment. |
| **AI on-ramp** | Months 2-4. Customer sees the prompt-edit experience, asks what else we do. | $249-$399 hosting \+ Voice CRM ($99) \= $348-$498/mo | Voice CRM is the lightest add-on with the most obvious ROI. |

That blended ARPU plus the 12-month hosting commitment makes the LTV math work even with relatively small acquisition volume.*

# **11\. Institutional Pricing Notes**

Institutional pricing is intentionally presented as ranges and "from" pricing rather than fixed published numbers. Three reasons:

* **Scope variance.** An institutional engagement can range from $25K to $500K depending on compliance requirements, integrations, and support intensity. Fixed published pricing would either underprice the high end or scare off the low end.

* **Procurement compatibility.** Institutional buyers go through procurement, RFPs, or contract committees. They expect a discovery process and a custom proposal. Fixed published pricing actually slows institutional sales rather than accelerating them.

* **Anchor protection.** Publishing institutional rates on the same site as $149/mo SMB hosting creates the same pricing-anchor problem the brand decision was designed to avoid. Institutional ranges are referenced; specifics happen in conversation.

The institutional discovery process: 30-minute initial conversation (free) — paid scoping engagement (typical $5,000, refundable against project fee) — written proposal with fixed scope, sequence, and pricing. The IEEE, Alvernia, and Cambridge Redevelopment Authority engagements follow this pattern. Chris owns institutional discovery; senior Esteemed Colleagues are pulled in for technical depth as needed.

# **13\. Open Questions and Decisions Pending**

* **Service delivery capacity.** Esteemed Colleagues network covers technical talent. SEO, email marketing, and social media management require different skill profiles. Do we deliver these in-house, partner with agencies under white-label, or pull from a different talent pool? This is the most important operational question and it gates the launch of these service lines.

* **Trident pricing alignment.** Trident is sold directly via cetaceanlabs.com to developers and enterprise customers. The pricing for Trident-powered services through Esteemed Digital should be consistent with the direct Trident pricing in some defensible way (markup over wholesale, or coupling to managed services value). Worth confirming with the Trident product team.

* **Annual vs. monthly billing.** All pricing in this document is shown monthly. Standard practice is to offer 10-15% discount for annual prepay. Worth deciding whether to publish annual pricing alongside monthly or hold annual as a sales close incentive.

* **Volume / multi-site discounts.** Customers with multiple sites (franchises, multi-location nonprofits, association chapters) need a volume discount structure. Not addressed in v1 of this matrix.

* **Cancellation and downgrade policy.** 12-month hosting agreement is established. Cancellation policy after 12 months, downgrade rules between tiers, and prorating logic for mid-month changes are all undefined.

* **Discount authority.** What discount can a salesperson offer without approval? What requires Chris approval? Worth specifying before the sales motion scales beyond Chris-only.
